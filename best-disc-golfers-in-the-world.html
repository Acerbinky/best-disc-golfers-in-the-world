How to read it

This is an editorial cross-division ranking, not an official combined tour table. MPO and FPO do not share one competitive standings system, so the order weighs major wins, current form, official rankings and season points rather than comparing raw scores across divisions.

What counted

2026 results through the MVP Open x OTB, the final regular-season points event; PDGA Official World Rankings updated Aug. 12; and StatMando's 24-month ranking and 15-week power view. Career major totals count PDGA Open-division majors.
